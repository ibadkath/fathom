The **main functionality of Fathom** is essentially:

> **Automatically capture a meeting, turn it into a transcript, and use AI to turn that transcript into useful meeting notes and follow-ups.**

The core workflow is:

**Meeting → Recording/Capture → Transcript → AI Summary → Action Items → Search/Highlights/Sharing**

### The main features

1. **Meeting capture**

- Works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams.
- Fathom can automatically capture scheduled meetings based on your settings.
2. **Transcription**

- Converts the meeting conversation into a speaker-labeled transcript.
- Current Fathom supports transcription in many languages.
3. **AI meeting summary**

- After the meeting, AI generates a concise summary of what happened.
- Different summary templates can focus on sales calls, 1:1s, project updates, interviews, etc.
4. **Action items**

- Detects tasks discussed during the meeting and can identify who they're assigned to.
5. **Highlights**

- Important moments can be marked so you can quickly return to or share them later.
6. **Search & ask questions**

- You can find information across your meetings, and Fathom's newer experience includes AI interaction with meeting information.
7. **Sharing**

- Meeting summaries and recordings can be automatically shared with attendees, depending on settings.
8. **AI follow-up**

- It can generate follow-up emails based on the meeting's action items
