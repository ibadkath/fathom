**New User Walkthrough**

1. Open the deployed Fathom URL. No account or authentication is required.
2. On the dashboard, review the seeded recent meetings.
3. Click **Q3 product strategy** to open the meeting.
4. Press the audio player’s **Play** button.
5. Read the transcript while the recording plays. Click any transcript segment to jump to that timestamp.
6. Open the **Summary** tab to read the AI-generated meeting summary, topics, and decisions.
7. Open **Action items** and check off completed follow-ups.
8. Click the **Highlighter** icon beside an important transcript segment. It will appear in the **Highlights** tab.
9. Use the search field to search for terms such as `activation`, `onboarding`, or `roadmap`.
10. Change the AI template using the template dropdown, such as **Sales call**, **Engineering**, or **Interview**.
11. Use **Ask this meeting** to ask a question, for example: `Who owns the next step?`
12. Click **Share**.
13. Choose **Selected clip** and click **Create share link**.
14. Open the generated public link in another browser or private window. The recipient can play the clip and read its transcript without being part of the meeting.

For the walkthrough, explain that meeting capture and the recording bot are intentionally stubbed with seeded meeting data; the post-meeting workflow is the primary MVP experience.
